# Native paid-entry experiments

`web-chain/native-experiments.html` connects owner-authored native posts, finite sponsored-index budgets and observed native costs. It measures paid index entries, exact publisher charges, unspent/refunded escrow, retained storage and explicitly supplied complete FINAL fee graphs. Human impressions, clicks, acquisition, revenue and causal lift remain unknown. This is a bounded native cost experiment, not audience measurement or a statistical proof that one message caused a business outcome.

The owner first publishes two distinct immutable native posts and locally preregisters exact A/B campaign identities, content commitments, prices, maximum entries and expiry. Preregistration sends nothing. A needs its own owner funding approval. B requires a measured, known paid A campaign and another owner approval. Iteration requires measurements for both exact original campaign commitments; the owner explicitly chooses a variant and approves a new campaign for its source/price. No model is needed to propose these owner-authored copies, choose an audience or decide the next action. The restricted shipped promotion worker performs each bounded placement separately.

The reader pins promotion/feed/company code and actual company ownership at one FINAL block. It recomputes immutable post hashes, campaign commitments, sequential placement receipt commitments, callback state and budget conservation. Known fee inputs are limited to selected campaign actions, fixed owner/operator accounts and complete original FINAL graphs with checked historical block identity. The report explicitly labels those fees as a subset; missing network fees are not assumed zero. Current RPC/code pins are trusted boundaries and do not establish historical code identity or light-client verification.

The fixed comparison criterion is publisher charge per paid native index entry. Retained storage and known gas fees are separately visible, so a lower publisher price is not presented as lower total business cost. A stopped/failed/pending native transfer is not a paid entry. Pending payment/refund uncertainty blocks a decision that requires a completed measurement. The existing promotion contract still enforces each actual source, publisher, price, storage quote, finite budget, expiry, owner and restricted signing key.

Measurement and decision hashes are retained in local public review history alongside exact native transaction hashes. The existing `fund` ABI commits its typed campaign authority; it does **not** independently attest the entire measurement report. The page states that distinction. Restored reports are untrusted data until validated, and current native records are re-read before approval. The UI never persists post text, private keys or credentials. It journals only public experiment/campaign metadata and original transaction identity. Cancel/account/epoch/review changes cannot resurrect an approval through wallet awaits; an uncertain dispatch is reconciled by its original hash and never automatically resubmitted.

The pure producer/validator is `web-chain/src/native-experiment-actions.js`. Its tests are `node --test web-chain/tests/native-experiment-actions.test.mjs`. They use synthetic public-state/FINAL fixtures and cover native accounting, independent commitments, foreign/inconsistent metrics, separate A/B/iteration decisions and retained uncertainty. Actual acceptance is the separate `integration/native_growth_e2e.mjs` local NEAR/Chrome run documented in [NATIVE_MONITORING.md](NATIVE_MONITORING.md). Do not infer a real-chain pass from unit fixtures or a source/build preflight.

This component does not randomize humans, track personal data, connect an external ads platform, claim measured acquisition, deploy publicly or move real funds during implementation acceptance. Native sponsored placement is real in the local protocol fixture; the audience outcomes remain unmeasured.
