# Native business requirements

The project accepts a business goal, proposes bounded work, requires the owner's approvals and records authority, budgets, task states and approved settlement on NEAR. AI inference is off-chain through a compatible provider; local Gemma is an explicit mock, not NEAR AI Cloud or verified confidential execution.

The [requirements baseline](native-business-requirements.json) retains44stable capability IDs. The [native acceptance catalogue](near-native-capabilities.json) maps each area to local cases, immutable historical component reports, explicit gaps and optional external extensions. A requirement is not an implemented feature. Historical source-pinned evidence remains historical after editorial or code changes; it does not become evidence of the current build, public wallet integration, live business outcomes or human hiring.

This editorial publication replaces a private research inventory with neutral project requirements. The original dated records and prior public artifacts are preserved privately. The cleanup makes no claim of independent creation, legal clearance or erasure of earlier repository history.

## Scope and evidence

Keep all44areas, their current statuses, missing work and acceptance conditions. Native inbox, publishing and payment surfaces are the selected target. Named external email/social/card providers remain optional extensions, with their own permissions and costs. Recurring operation requires an available worker; contract state is not a clock. Owner approval, budget limits, freeze, unknown-original recovery, recipient binding and source/receipt integrity remain mandatory.

Two predecessor component reports include source paths retired by this editorial change. Their exact original receipt hashes, checks and whole source-inventory identities stay pinned. Those reports are not rewritten or renamed as current proof. A fresh source inventory requires a new reviewed component; missing retired sources remain source drift, never implicit acceptance.

`python scripts/verify_native_requirements.py --structure-only` checks only the exact44-ID baseline/catalogue link and selected hashes. It does not execute a workflow or accept features. The default command additionally audits the declared immutable component reports when they are available and exits2 when evidence is missing, stale or incomplete. This public source export may omit historical private report files; missing evidence must remain visible rather than silently accepted.

Production company/goal integration, general autonomous management, live human outcomes, production wallet recovery and hosted availability need their own exact acceptance. Repeated tests of bounded components do not establish a complete public business workflow.
