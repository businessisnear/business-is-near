      What is running in this demo?

      This is a working prototype on public NEAR testnet. AI text generation uses a pinned local Gemma 4 model through the NEAR AI-compatible client contract. The worker runs separately on an operator computer; continuous hosting is not guaranteed.

      NEAR AI Cloud billing, TEE execution and mainnet are unverified. The demonstration company uses operator-held testnet keys. Visitors have read-only access to that company; its keys are never supplied to this site.

      This public build focuses on content and website drafts. Private media and document services, inbox, bookings and the other operator tools are available in the downloadable project and recorded local demonstration; they are not connected here.

      Start your own testnet company through the guided wallet setup. It creates the company account, encrypted brief, task capacity and owner-approved AI policy. Worker availability is checked from its current on-chain heartbeat and company-specific restricted permission before new approvals.
